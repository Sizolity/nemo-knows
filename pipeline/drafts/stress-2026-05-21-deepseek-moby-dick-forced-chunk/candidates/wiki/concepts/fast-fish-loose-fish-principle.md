# Fast Fish Loose Fish Principle

The fast-fish loose-fish principle names a whaling custom that dictates ownership of a whale. A “fast fish” is harpooned and still attached to the hunting boat by a line—it belongs to the vessel that holds it. A “loose fish” is unclaimed, whether swimming free or dead, and becomes the property of any ship that can secure it. In *Moby‑Dick* the rule exemplifies the category of [[whaling-life-practices-and-customs]], where economic codes and the profit motive override mercy, treating both whale and men as entries on a balance sheet.
