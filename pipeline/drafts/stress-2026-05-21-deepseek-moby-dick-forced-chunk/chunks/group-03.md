## Cross-Chunk Summary
The group opens amidst the butchering of a sperm whale. Stubb completes his mockery of the cook Fleece, the crew battles sharks, and a full description of cutting‑in unrolls. The stripped carcass becomes a floating ghost; its severed head provokes Ahab’s Sphynx address. An encounter with the plague‑stricken *Jeroboam* and its fanatic prophet Gabriel sharpens the sense of ominous forces. The head‑to‑head comparison of sperm and right whales yields anatomical and symbolic contrasts, and Tashtego’s near‑drowning in the Heidelburgh Tun of spermaceti is followed by phrenological musings on the whale’s tiny brain. The chase with the German *Jungfrau* ends with a sinking whale, then digressions elevate whalemen to mythic status (Perseus, St. George, Vishnoo), dissect the spout and tail, and defend Jonah. Heading towards the Straits of Sunda, the *Pequod* enters the vast “Grand Armada” of sperm whales, pursued by Malay pirates. Calm nursing females and calves appear in the herd’s centre. Chapters on whale schools, fast‑fish/loose‑fish law, English royal prerogative, and Stubb’s ambergris trick on the *Rose‑Bud* follow. The closing notes describe the origin of the whale’s malodorous reputation and introduce Pip, whose two jumpings from Stubb’s boat culminate in oceanic abandonment and a broken mind.

## Repeated Or Central Claims
- **Whale anatomy as boundary and mystery**: The blubber is a “blanket” with indecipherable line‑engravings; the sperm‑whale forehead is a dead, blind wall of boneless toughness; eyes give two independent fields of vision with blind zones; the spout’s precise nature remains an open question; the tail’s five motions express an unknowable intelligence. Phrenology and physiognomy are applied but found wanting—the brain is minuscule, the brow inscrutable.
- **Economic and social determinism over mercy**: Stubb’s mockery of Fleece, his threat that Pip is worth less than a whale, and the fast‑fish/loose‑fish code all foreground a world where possession, profit, and custom override tenderness. The English royal‑fish law and Stubb’s manipulation of the *Rose‑Bud* captain extend the logic of appropriation.
- **Whale as source of paradox and rich utility**: The animal that gives light is considered foul to eat; the sperm whale yields perfumery‑grade ambergris from disease; its oil can be nearly scentless when tried at sea; even its “blanket” contains sacred, untranslatable marks.
- **Crew as microcosm of fate and interdependence**: The monkey‑rope ties Ishmael to Queequeg in a “joint‑stock company of mortality”; the schoolmaster bull and harems mirror social order; Pip’s fragile sensibility is sacrificed to the chase and later becomes a vessel of prophetic madness.

- **Mythological and legal frameworks**: Whaling honour linked to Perseus, St. George, Hercules, Vishnoo; Jonah defended with multiple rationalisations; fast‑fish/loose‑fish expanded into a social‑political metaphor; English Crown’s head‑and‑tail prerogative satirized for its anatomical blunder (whalebone in head).
- **Character moments**: Ahab’s Sphynx soliloquy; Tashtego’s “running delivery” rescue by Queequeg; Stubb and Flask speculating that Fedallah is the devil aiming to kidnap Ahab; Pip’s first rescue and then his abandonment, with Stubb’s grim economic calculus.
