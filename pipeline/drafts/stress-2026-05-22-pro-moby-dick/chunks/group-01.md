## Repeated Or Central Claims
- **Dignity of whaling**: Whaling is unjustly scorned yet historically vital (exploration, economics, light‑bearing), and whalemen provide royal anointing oil (sperm oil in coronations). The whale‑ship discovered new lands and carried missionaries.
- **The sea as truth, land as peril**: Land represents safety and spiritual slavery; the open ocean is where “landlessness alone resides highest truth.” The lee shore paradox recurs in Ishmael’s meditations.
- **Queequeg’s nobility**: Despite his “cannibal” origin, Queequeg possesses innate dignity, a “simple honest heart,” and a joint‑stock philosophy. His cross‑cultural friendship with Ishmael overcomes initial prejudice.
- **Religious tolerance and its limits**: Ishmael reasons that doing God’s will (golden rule) justifies joining Queequeg’s idol‑worship, but later dismisses Ramadan as harmful nonsense. Father Mapple’s sermon makes Jonah a model of repentance and a warning against fleeing divine truth.
- **Ahab’s foreshadowed monomania**: Before appearing, Ahab is described by Peleg as “a grand, ungodly, god‑like man” and by Elijah as ill‑fated; his scar, ivory leg, auger hole stance, pipe‑throwing, and white‑whale command all point to a consuming quest.
- **The crew as “Isolatoes”**: Men from disparate races and continents, united only by the keel, reflect the ship as a federated microcosm.
- **Contrast and identity**: Ishmael frequently states that things are known only through their opposites—warmth through cold, self through darkness—a recurring epistemological theme.
- **The whale’s mystery and chaos of cetology**: The Extracts are explicitly unreliable; Ishmael’s formal Cetology chapter begins by bemoaning the “impenetrable veil” and “utter confusion” of classification.

## Important Local Details
- **Etymology and Extracts**: The word “whale” traces to roundness/rolling. The Sub‑Sub‑Librarian’s hodge‑podge of quotations is presented as entertainment, not science.
- **Spouter‑Inn**: Contains a puzzling oil painting (a whale impaling itself on three mast‑heads), whale‑jaw bar, and scrimshaw. Landlord Peter Coffin teases Ishmael about the harpooneer “peddling his head” (selling embalmed New Zealand heads).
- **Queequeg’s appearance and ritual**: Dark‑square tattooing, bald head with scalp‑knot, “purplish yellow” skin; worships a small ebony idol Yojo, fasts in a squat, shaves with a harpoon, puts on boots under the bed.
- **The bedding scene**: Ishmael’s terror turns to peaceful sleep beside Queequeg, leading to the aphorism “Better sleep with a sober cannibal than a drunken Christian.” In the morning, Queequeg’s tattooed arm draped over Ishmael triggers a childhood memory.
- **Whaleman’s Chapel**: Marble tablets memorialise men lost at sea; Father Mapple enters through a storm, mounts the pulpit via a rope ladder he then pulls up (spiritual isolation), and preaches Jonah: sin as a crooked chamber, deliverance through the “great fish.”
- **Nantucket and Try Pots**: Barren sand‑island whose people rule the oceans; innkeeper Hosea Hussey serves perpetual clam and cod chowder; a past accident forbids harpoons in bedrooms.
- **Pequod’s description**: Weather‑darkened, aged, ornamented with whale‑bone and teeth, a tiller carved from a jaw, a wigwam of right‑whale jaw‑bones on deck; “all noble things are touched” with melancholy.
- **Lay system**: Whalemen paid in shares of net profits. Ishmael gets the 300th lay after bartering with Peleg and Bildad; Queequeg demonstrates harpoon accuracy and receives the 90th lay; Bildad quotes “Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth” to justify a 777th offer.
- **Captains Peleg and Bildad**: Peleg is blustery and generous, fiercely loyal to Ahab; Bildad is a miserly “fighting Quaker” who reads the Bible while bargaining and drives crews hard without swearing.
- **Elijah’s warnings**: A pock‑marked stranger hints at a Cape Horn ordeal, a skrimmage in Santa, a silver calabash, and a prophecy about Ahab’s leg; reappears with ominous questions, reinforcing a sense of fate.
- **Ahab’s entry and habits**: Marked by a livid, rod‑like whitish scar; ivory leg from sperm whale jaw; stands in an auger hole; throws his pipe overboard when it fails to comfort; first shouted command is to watch for the white whale.
- **Mates’ personalities**: Starbuck’s courage is pragmatic and not rash, vetoing any man “not afraid of a whale,” yet susceptible to spiritual terror. Stubb treats everything with pipe‑smoking good humour and later dream‑rationalises Ahab’s kick as an honour. Flask regards whales as overgrown vermin.
- **Harpooneers**: Queequeg (Starbuck’s), Tashtego (unmixed Gay Head Indian, Stubb’s), Daggoo (gigantic African, Flask’s).
- **Stubb’s dream**: Transforms Ahab’s kick into an honour from a great man with an ivory leg, encouraged by a hump‑backed figure; reflects the crew’s absorption of Ahab’s authority.
- **Cetology opening**: Ishmael quotes Scoresby (“impenetrable veil”) and Beale (“utter confusion”) as he begins his own classification, framing the effort as necessary but fraught.
