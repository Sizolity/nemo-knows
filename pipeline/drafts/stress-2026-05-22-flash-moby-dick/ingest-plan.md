# Ingest Plan

## Source Summary
- Full text of Melville’s *Moby‑Dick*, retrieved from Project Gutenberg via curl fallback after a TLS failure; includes YAML front‑matter with fetch metadata.
- Narrated by Ishmael, the voyage of the Pequod (Nantucket) under Captain Ahab, who obsessively hunts the white whale that took his leg; blends narrative with digressive treatises on cetology, whiteness, whaling law, and the philosophical limits of knowledge.
- Central motifs: Ahab’s monomania and the “pasteboard mask” philosophy, the uncanny terror of whiteness, inescapable fate vs. chance, and the human cost of pursuit—embodied in Queequeg’s friendship, Pip’s abandonment, and the Town‑Ho inset.
- The novel is a compendium of whaling practices, cetological classification, legal codes (Fast‑Fish/Loose‑Fish), and sailor‑art, all filtered through Ishmael’s ironic, encyclopedic voice.
