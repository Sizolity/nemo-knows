# The Time Machine

## What It Is

## Summary
*The Time Machine* is an 1895 science fiction novella. A Victorian scientist, the Time Traveller, journeys to the year 802,701 and finds humanity evolved into two distinct species: the childlike Eloi and the savage Morlocks. The work popularized the concept of time travel, coined the term "time machine," and explores class divisions and social inequality.

## Key Claims
- Popularized the concept of time travel and coined the term "time machine."
- Depicts a distant future where humanity has diverged into two species (Eloi and Morlocks).
- Explores themes of class divisions and social inequality.
