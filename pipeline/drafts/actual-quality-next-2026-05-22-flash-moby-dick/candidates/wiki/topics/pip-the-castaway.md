# Pip The Castaway

In the incomplete text of *Moby‑Dick*, the black ship‑keeper Pip becomes the novel’s castaway after a panic‑stricken whale chase. His boat‑crew abandons him twice; the second time, he drifts alone on the open sea. The retrieved manuscript breaks off mid‑sentence as his rescue begins.

Pip also surfaces in the economic arithmetic of the whale‑fishery. [[moby-dick-characters|Stubb]]’s offhand appraisal of the boy’s cash value shows how the logic of possession swallows a human life alongside bone and blubber.
