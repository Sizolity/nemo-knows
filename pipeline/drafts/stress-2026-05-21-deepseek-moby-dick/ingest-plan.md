# Ingest Plan

## Source Summary
- Full plain-text of Herman Melville’s _Moby-Dick; or, The Whale_ (1851) obtained from Project Gutenberg.
- Narrative source combining a whaling voyage with encyclopedic digressions on cetology, whaling practices, and philosophical reflections.
- Dominated by themes of obsession (Ahab’s hunt for the white whale), the sublime terror of whiteness, and the democracy of life aboard the Pequod.
- Includes etymologies, extracts, and 135 chapters followed by an epilogue.
