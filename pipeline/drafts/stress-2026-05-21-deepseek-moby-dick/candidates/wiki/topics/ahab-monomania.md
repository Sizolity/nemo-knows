# Ahab Monomania

In *Moby‑Dick*, Captain Ahab’s monomania manifests as an all‑consuming obsession with a single quarry: the [[whiteness-of-the-whale|white sperm whale]] that maimed him. His fixation overrides every other consideration aboard the *Pequod*, transforming the whaling voyage into a personal vendetta.
