# Cetology In Moby Dick

Ishmael’s first‑person narrative is punctuated by systematic whale lore: the full Project Gutenberg text preserves the chapters where he sorts whales into folio, octavo, and duodecimo groups and anatomises them in detail. These digressions sit beside the driving plot of [[ahab-monomania|Ahab’s obsessive quest]] for the white sperm whale, a creature whose symbolic charge is later taken up in treatments of [[whiteness-of-the-whale|whiteness]]. The interplay between scientific observation and the novel’s metaphysical reach turns the *Pequod*’s voyage into both a whaling manual and a philosophical inquiry.
