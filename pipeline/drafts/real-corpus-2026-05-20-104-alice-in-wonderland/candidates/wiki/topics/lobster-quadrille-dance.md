# Lobster Quadrille Dance

The **lobster quadrille** is a surreal dance featured in *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, where the Queen of Hearts orders a performance by lobsters dressed as soldiers. The scene epitomizes the world's absurdity, as the dance follows no logical choreography and serves merely to entertain a tyrannical ruler. Alice observes the spectacle from the sidelines, highlighting the disconnect between the characters' chaotic actions and their rigid adherence to arbitrary royal decrees.
