# Mushroom Wonderland Side Effects

In *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, the mushroom serves as a primary magical object that allows Alice to manipulate her physical dimensions. Unlike other items that have deterministic effects, such as the "DRINK ME" bottle which causes shrinking or the "EAT ME" label which initially failed to change size, the mushroom possesses dual properties. By consuming different sides of the same fungus, Alice can selectively grow larger or shrink smaller. This mechanism is central to her navigation of a world governed by **[[wonderland-size-manipulation]]**, where fluid identity and rapid physical changes destabilize her sense of self. The ability to control her height via this object stands in contrast to other nonsensical elements like the **[[wonderland-arbitrary-authority]]** that governs the rest of the narrative.
