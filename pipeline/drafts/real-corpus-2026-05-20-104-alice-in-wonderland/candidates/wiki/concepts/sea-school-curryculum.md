# Sea School Curryculum

The **Sea School Curriculum** refers to the structured yet absurd educational framework within *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*. It is characterized by a complete rejection of conventional logic, where students must navigate surreal geography and fluid identities to survive.

## Core Principles

- **Fluid Identity**: Learners frequently question their own nature, destabilizing their sense of self as they shift between shrinking and growing states.
- **Inverted Logic**: Mathematical facts are incorrect (e.g., 4 × 5 = 12), and cause-and-effect relationships defy real-world physics.
- **Madness as Norm**: The curriculum assumes that everyone is mad, rendering the definition of sanity circular and subjective.
- **Arbitrary Authority**: Rules are invented on the spot or overridden by decrees such as "Off with her head!", rather than logical reasoning.

## Instructional Methods

- **Magic Objects**: Specific items have deterministic effects on physical state: a "DRINK ME" bottle causes shrinking, while mushroom sides control height changes.
- **Nonsensical Rules**: Events follow internal logic that contradicts reality, such as the **caucus-race-nonsense** where everyone runs arbitrarily and everyone wins, or painting white roses red to avoid execution.
- **Dream Frame**: The entire narrative is revealed to be a dream experienced by Alice during a nap, framing the education as an illusion.

## Key Locations

The curriculum takes place in locations like the Duchess's nursery, where a baby turns into a pig, and the Croquet-Ground, where gardeners manipulate nature with absurd tasks.
