"The Republic" by Plato is a Socratic dialogue written around 375 BC.
Through conversations between Socrates and various Athenians, the work
explores the meaning of justice and whether the just person is happier
than the unjust. Socrates examines existing forms of government and
proposes an ideal city-state ruled by philosopher-kings. The dialogue
ranges across profound questions: the nature of the soul, the role of
poetry, love, aging, and the purpose of political [ ] power itself.
