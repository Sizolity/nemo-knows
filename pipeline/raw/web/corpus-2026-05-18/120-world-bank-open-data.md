## Retrieved Text

This site is designed to make World Bank data easy to find, download, and use. All of the data found here can be used free of charge with minimal restrictions.

To get started, users can access individual data series and entire datasets through search, browsing, download, DataBank, widgets, APIs, the data catalog, and the Microdata Library.

Search Bar and Site Menu

The easiest way to look for data is to use the search box at the top of the page. Users can search for indicator names, countries, and topics. The search box shows matching results as text is entered; selecting a matching term displays the result. Users can look at combinations of indicators and countries or multiple countries by adding additional items in the search box, though only one indicator can be displayed at a time. The site also lets users browse countries, topics, and indicators from the menu.

Download

Data pages allow users to download displayed data in bulk. Country pages provide all data for all years for a single country, while indicator pages provide data for all countries for all years. This supports desktop analysis in tools such as Excel.

DataBank

Data displayed on the site are a subset of the collections available in the World Bank's DataBank. DataBank contains extensive time-series datasets and supports selecting, slicing, customized queries, downloads, charts, and visualizations.

Widgets

Widgets are snippets of code for embedding data charts or maps on another website. Widgets are dynamic and update when new data become available.

Access data through Web API

The Data API lets websites and tools access data directly. It is intended for custom data visualizations, live combinations with other data sources, mashups, and related uses. data.worldbank.org is itself built using data through the Data API.

Data catalog

The Data Catalog provides a comprehensive listing of data and datasets published by the World Bank. It includes datasets in DataBank and other datasets such as sub-national and raw data from surveys. Datasets can be downloaded or accessed through DataBank or dataset-specific tools.

Micro data

The World Bank Microdata Library facilitates access to microdata collected through sample surveys of households, businesses, and other facilities. These data describe people living in developing countries, institutions, environments, communities, and economies. Many datasets are available as open data subject to their terms of use.

Open Data at the World Bank

The World Bank recognizes transparency and accountability as essential to development and to its mission to alleviate poverty. Its commitment to openness is also driven by the desire to foster public ownership, partnership, and participation. As a knowledge institution, the World Bank shares knowledge freely and openly.

Statistics and data are a key part of that knowledge and are accessible on the web. The World Bank provides free and open access to development data about countries around the globe, together with other datasets cited in the data catalog.

Broader access helps policymakers and advocacy groups make better-informed decisions and measure improvements more accurately. The data also support research by journalists, academia, and others.
